Download for macOS Source

Install in 3 steps

  1. Open the DMG, drag KeyRemapper to Applications.
  2. macOS may block it once — System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. It needs Hammerspoon — don't have it? The app walks you through it.

Click a key on the virtual keyboard — or press it — type what it should output, hit Save. Your remaps land in ~/.hammerspoon/keyremaps.lua and reload instantly.
